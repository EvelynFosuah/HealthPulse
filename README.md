# HealthPulse
Personal health-tracking application exploring how software can help individuals monitor health information.

Health data often lives in scattered places: a doctor's printout, a fitness app, a note on a phone. HealthPulse is a project about bringing that information together so people can record it, see how it changes over time, and walk into appointments with a clearer picture of their own health.

Disclaimer: HealthPulse is an educational and exploratory project. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.

 *Features*
 
- Health metric logging: record measurements such as weight, blood pressure, heart rate, sleep, and temperature.
- Trends and charts: visualize how readings change over days, weeks, and months.
- Medication and appointment tracking: keep schedules and notes in one place.
- Symptom journal: log how you feel, with dates and optional notes, to spot patterns.
- Reminders: gentle prompts for medications, check-ins, and upcoming appointments.
- Exportable summaries: generate a simple report to share with a clinician.
